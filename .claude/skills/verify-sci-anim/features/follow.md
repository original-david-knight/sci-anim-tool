# Follow a law or carry along a curve

A scene file's `follow` animation sets an object's position, rotation, scale or object opacity, or the camera's position or target, at every scene time of its span from a law: `law` writes `x`, `y` and `z` as expressions in `t`, law time in seconds, or for `sets: opacity` the one expression `value`, and `sets` names the value. `from` counts law time from a chapter's start, so follows in several steps continue one motion. A follow may instead set `along`, the object id of a flat shape, plot or tube, and carry its object along that curve by length over its span, turning it to face along the curve with `faces: tangent`. `inspect` reports a followed object's transform as usual, `sci_anim stages` reports it at every frame time between two scene times, and `validate` refuses a follow that does not start where its value is.

## Sub-features

- `follow-law` sets a rotation and a position from a law, and holds what the law gave at the span's end.
- `follow-from` continues one law across two steps of a chapter with `from`, with no jump between them.
- `follow-camera` circles the camera with `sets: camera-position`.
- `follow-opacity-scale` pulses a pattern in `scale` and fades it out by an `opacity` law while a text's opacity law takes it to 0.2, holds both after the span, refuses an opacity law that leaves 0 to 1 (`out_of_range` at `value`), one that starts away from the opacity or scale (`law_jump`), and `x`, `y`, `z` in an opacity law, and judges faded text in `check-layout` as it judges text a fade faded.
- `follow-carry` carries a triangle along a plot while a `create` draws it, on the pen's end and facing along the curve, and along a shape that moves or is under a linear map.
- `follow-errors` refuses a law that starts away from its value (`law_jump`), an expression with no value at a frame time (`out_of_range` with the scene time), unknown names in the law (`bad_format` at each expression), an `easing_curve` with a law, a `from` naming a chapter that has not begun, a follow of a plot (`placed_by_axes`), a carry that does not start where its object stands or faces (`carry_jump` with the value to declare), `along` text (`no_curve`), and `law` with `along`.
- `follow-stages` prints the stage at every frame time between `--from` and `--to` with `sci_anim stages`.
- `follow-layout` runs `check-layout`, which checks every frame time of each follow's span, leaves them out of `stages.txt` and the contact sheet, and skips them with `--ends-only`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Write an animation with `"type": "follow"` in a play step, then run `validate`, `inspect`, `stages`, `frame`, `render`, `frames` or `check-layout` on the scene file.
- `examples/laws.json` orbits a planet across a chapter with `from`, circles the camera around a cut-open cone, and carries a triangle along a plot as it is created. `skills/sci-anim/examples/pendulum.json` swings a rod and its bob, each by its own law.
- `sci_anim schema | jq '.["$defs"].AnimationFile.allOf[].then | select(.properties.type.const == "follow")'` prints the fields, and `jq '.["$defs"].LawFile'` the law's.

## Gotchas

- A law gives the value outright. Declare the object where the law starts, or the scene file has `law_jump`.
- A follow of a pattern's `scale` scales the whole ring about the pattern's `position`, so the copies draw in as they shrink. That is the transform's scale, not each copy's.
- A carry sets the whole position, z included: an object carried along a curve at z = 0 sits at z = 0, so it cannot be nudged toward the camera.
- Angles in a law's `rotation` are degrees, but the functions read radians: `30*cos(2.21*t)` swings 30 degrees at 2.21 radians a second.
- `inspect`'s rotation of an object carried along a tube with `faces` can change fast where its own z axis points along the world's x axis, where Euler angles lose one of their three; the frame draws it turning smoothly.
- A follow's frame times make `check-layout` slower by the span's length times the frame rate.
